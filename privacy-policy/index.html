This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Datalix collects when you use our website to buy mobile data bundles, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, the phone number to receive the data bundle, and an email address if you choose to provide one.
  • Transaction data: product purchased, amount paid, order reference, and payment status. We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, or Mobile Money PIN — these go directly to Paystack.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically to keep the site working properly and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your order (sending the data bundle to the number you provided).
  • To send you a payment receipt or order updates, if you gave us an email address.
  • To respond to support requests and resolve payment or delivery issues.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to comply with legal obligations.

3. How your information is shared

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Paystack, to process your payment securely. Paystack's own terms and privacy practices govern how it handles your payment details.
  • Service providers who help us run the site (for example, hosting), under obligations to keep your data secure.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data retention

We keep order records for as long as reasonably needed to handle support queries, refunds, accounting, and legal requirements, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

5. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana, you have the right to ask us what personal information we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate information, and to request deletion where we are not required to keep it (for example, for accounting purposes). To exercise these rights, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No online service can guarantee complete security, and payment card data is handled entirely by Paystack's PCI-DSS compliant systems, not by Datalix.

7. Children

Datalix is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].
