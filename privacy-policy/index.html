Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Datalix collects when you use our website to buy mobile data bundles, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the information you provide at checkout: your name, the recipient phone number for the data bundle, and, if you choose to provide it, your email address. When you pay, Paystack collects and processes your card or Mobile Money payment details directly — we do not receive or store your full card number or Mobile Money PIN.

We also automatically collect basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, for security and troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your data bundle order
  • To send you an order confirmation and, where an email was provided, a receipt
  • To verify payment with our payment partners
  • To respond to support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud

3. Sharing Your Information

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Paystack — to process your payment securely.
  • Our order-processing systems — to record and fulfill your order.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Data Retention

We keep order records for as long as reasonably necessary for accounting, fraud-prevention, and customer-support purposes, after which they may be deleted or anonymized.

5. Your Rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct inaccurate information, or to delete information we no longer need to keep. To make a request, contact us using the details on our website.

6. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies needed for the site and checkout to function correctly. We do not currently use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for users who are able to enter into a binding contract under the laws of Ghana. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through the details listed on our website.
