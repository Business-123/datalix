Refund Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

We want every Datalix order to arrive successfully. This Refund Policy explains when a refund applies and how to request one.

1. Non-Delivery of a Data Bundle

If you paid for a data bundle and it was not delivered to the recipient number within a reasonable time, contact us with your order reference (and, if you have one, the payment reference from Paystack). We will investigate and, where the order genuinely failed to deliver and is confirmed as SUCCESS on our side but not received on the network, we will either resend the bundle or issue a full refund to your original payment method.

2. Incorrect Recipient Number Supplied by the Customer

Because data bundles are delivered automatically to the phone number entered at checkout, orders sent to an incorrect number due to customer error are not eligible for a refund once the bundle has been delivered to that number. Please double-check the recipient number before completing payment. If you notice an error immediately after paying and before the bundle has been delivered, contact us right away and we will try to correct it if fulfillment hasn't happened yet.

3. Failed or Duplicate Payments

If Paystack charged you but no order was created on our site, or you were charged twice for the same order, contact us with proof of payment (such as a Paystack receipt or Mobile Money confirmation SMS). We will verify the payment with Paystack and refund any amount that was charged without a corresponding order.

4. Out-of-Stock or Unavailable Products

Products marked "Out of Stock" cannot be purchased. If, in rare cases, an order is accepted for a product that turns out to be unavailable, we will notify you and issue a full refund.

5. How to Request a Refund

Contact us through the details listed on our website with:

  • Your order reference number
  • The phone number used for the order
  • A brief description of the issue

We aim to review refund requests within a few business days.

6. Refund Method and Timing

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method through Paystack. Depending on your card issuer or Mobile Money provider, it can take several business days for a refund to reflect after it has been processed on our end.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact Us

For any refund or order issue, please reach out through the contact details listed on our website, or reply to your order confirmation email.
