Refund Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Data bundles are digital goods that are delivered almost immediately after payment, so our approach to refunds is different from that of a physical product store. This policy explains when a refund is available.

1. Successfully delivered bundles

Once a data bundle has been successfully delivered to the phone number you provided at checkout, the purchase is final and non-refundable. This includes cases where you entered the wrong phone number or wrong network, changed your mind after purchase, or did not use the data before it expired (bundle validity is set by the network operator, not Datalix).

2. When you are entitled to a refund

You are entitled to a full refund if:

  • Paystack charged you but Datalix did not deliver the data bundle within [e.g. "24 hours"] of payment, and the delay is not caused by the network operator; or
  • You were charged more than once for the same order (duplicate charge); or
  • You were charged but your payment shows as failed, declined, or abandoned in our system.

3. When a refund is not available

  • The bundle was delivered to the number you entered, even if that number was incorrect.
  • Delivery was delayed or affected by an outage, congestion, or other issue caused by MTN, AirtelTigo, or Telecel rather than Datalix.
  • You simply no longer want the bundle after it has been delivered.

4. How to request a refund

Contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] or [SUPPORT PHONE/WHATSAPP] with your order reference (shown on your payment confirmation page) and a short description of the issue. We aim to review refund requests within [e.g. "2 business days"].

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method through Paystack. Depending on your bank or Mobile Money provider, it can take a few business days for the refund to appear in your account.

6. Failed or declined payments

If your payment fails or is declined on Paystack's page, you are not charged and no order is created, so there is nothing to refund — you can simply try again.

7. Contact

For any question about a payment or a refund, reach us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].
